# Code of conduct

Tyrminal is built in the open, and everyone taking part is expected to make it a respectful,
harassment-free place.

## Expected behaviour

- Be considerate and constructive; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before assuming.
- Respect differing experience levels, backgrounds and viewpoints.
- Keep discussions on topic and accept decisions made in the project's documented process.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption.
- Publishing someone else's private information without permission.
- Sexualised language or imagery, or unwelcome advances.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may remove, edit or reject contributions and comments that break this code, and
may temporarily or permanently ban participants who do.

> **Contact for conduct reports: not yet decided (open question for the maintainer).** Until
> one is published here, open an issue asking the maintainer for a private channel, without
> details.

Reports will be handled privately, and reporters will not face retaliation.
